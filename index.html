HOW TO PLAY

Find your aim.

Touch or click near David’s sling. Drag backward and downward, then release. The dotted line shows where the stone may fly.

Hit the golden emblem on the shield. Later stages add moving targets, stone walls, wind, and fictional shadow birds approaching from different directions that can catch a flying stone. Wait for it to pass or aim around it. David chose five smooth stones, so you have five throws per challenge. After a miss, the optional guide shows your previous flight path—adjust your pull and try again.
