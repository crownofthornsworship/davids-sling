# David's Sling — Improvement Roadmap

## Product goal

Turn the 99-challenge precision game into a satisfying skill journey with readable physics, meaningful target variety, and fair difficulty rather than clutter or random obstruction.

## Phase 1 — Shot feel and layout

- Calibrate pull distance, trajectory preview, release timing, collision, and camera response.
- Keep popups, counters, and touch controls outside the aiming lane on portrait and landscape screens.
- Add immediate feedback for clean hits, glancing hits, blocked shots, and misses.
- Add quick retry, previous-shot ghost, and optional aim assistance for younger players.

## Phase 2 — Challenge structure

- Group 99 challenges into themed chapters with a mechanic introduced, practiced, then combined.
- Use moving shields, breakable walls, wind, ricochets, timed openings, and shadow birds predictably.
- Prevent impossible random combinations by using authored spawn patterns.
- Add three optional mastery goals per chapter without blocking story progress.

## Phase 3 — Art and motion

- Animate sling windup, rotation, release, stone flight, impact, target reaction, and celebration.
- Give sunrise, grove, and night valleys distinct foregrounds, atmosphere, and ambient animation.
- Improve walls, shields, birds, stones, and effects so their collision shapes are visually honest.

## Phase 4 — Set pieces

- Add rescue shots, moving-platform shots, multi-target puzzles, and short boss-style accuracy trials.
- Build a final Goliath-inspired courage challenge that is respectful, non-graphic, and mechanically earned.
- Add chapter map progression, medals, replay, and persistent accessibility settings.

## Release gate

- Verify all 99 challenges are completable without random failure.
- Test touch, mouse, portrait, landscape, tablet, and desktop; confirm no UI overlaps the launch area.
- Refresh social preview, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio.
